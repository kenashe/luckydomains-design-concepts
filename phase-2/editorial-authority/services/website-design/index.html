Website Design & Development

A website built to be found, understood, and trusted.

Lucky Domains designs and builds websites on your own domain, with the structure, performance, and technical foundation that search engines and AI systems rely on. Launch is the start of the site's job, not the end of ours.

IThe approach

Structure before style.

A good-looking site can still be invisible. Most of the problems we see are structural: pages that do not match what people search for, slow templates, duplicate URLs, missing structured data, and content no one can find from the home page.

We fix that at the foundation. Information architecture comes before visual design, and the technical work is built in from the first template, not added after launch.

Consult on the site you have

Ken inspects the existing site and writes down what is blocking search and conversion: structure, speed, indexing, page targeting, content gaps. You get a plain-English recommendation to fix or to rebuild, and what either would take.

Build or rebuild

Site structure, page templates, the core pages, and the technical foundation, launched on a real host and your own domain. Pages are built around what people actually search for, so the search work has something to stand on from day one.

IIIHow a project runs

Six steps, from discovery to improvement.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Goals, audience, the questions customers ask, and what the current site does and does not do.

  2. 02

    Structure

    A page map built from real search demand and the services you actually sell.

  3. 03

    Design

    Templates that make the business clear in seconds and work on every screen size.

  4. 04

    Build

    Clean, maintainable implementation with performance, accessibility, and structured data built in.

  5. 05

    Launch

    On your domain, with redirects, canonicals, Search Console, and analytics set up correctly.

  6. 06

    Improve

    Measure what people and search engines do with the site, and adjust.

IVThe foundation

What search engines and AI systems rely on.

  1. Information architecture One page per job, organized the way customers search.
  2. Performance Fast templates, compressed images, and nothing loaded that the page does not need.
  3. Accessibility Semantic markup, keyboard support, readable contrast, and motion that respects user settings.
  4. Technical SEO Clean URLs, self-referencing canonicals, sitemaps, and internal links that match them.
  5. Structured data Organization, Person, service, and breadcrumb data that describes real facts.
  6. Analytics foundations Measurement set up deliberately, with only what you need to make decisions.
  7. Conversion and trust Clear paths to contact, honest proof, and nothing that feels like a trap.
  8. Maintainability A handoff you can edit without us, documented in plain English.

What this is not: a general web shop, e-commerce platform maintenance, open-ended feature work, or a visual-only redesign with no search job behind it.
