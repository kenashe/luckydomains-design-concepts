IVEntity SEO & Digital Identity

Search engines organize the web around entities, not just keywords. Your identity should be unambiguous wherever it appears.

One clear, consistent identity for a person or a company, in plain words and in structured data.

  • Person and Organization structured data with stable identifiers
  • Official profile mapping and consistency

  • Knowledge Graph readiness assessment

VIWays to work together

Scoped in writing, before work starts.

Pricing is set after the consultation, from a written scope, not from a menu. Domain brokerage is success-based: if there is no purchase, there is no success fee.

  • Project

    A defined piece of work, such as a website build, an entity audit, or a domain acquisition, scoped in writing before work starts.

  • Ongoing

    Monthly search and visibility work, with a written scope and plain-English reporting. No ranking guarantees.

  • Full launch

    Name, site, search foundation, and identity together, for a new business or a rebrand.

What Lucky Domains is not. Not a full-service marketing agency; No paid ads or social media management; No open-ended retainers; No visual-only redesigns with no search job behind them. We do not guarantee rankings, AI citations, mentions in any particular AI system, or a Knowledge Panel. No one honestly can. We improve the evidence those systems work from, and we show you what changed.
