Phase 2 redesign concepts

Three new, light directions for LuckyDomains.io. They correct the business direction of Phase 1 and share one positioning, one set of services, and one content file, so the comparison is about design.

These are proposed design directions only. They do not modify LuckyDomains.io, KenAshe.ai, production repositories, DNS, forms, or integrations.

Why Phase 2

Phase 1 moved too far from the commercial business. It put AI experiments and a venture-studio identity at the center, when the work customers actually buy is websites, search, identity, and domains. Phase 2 starts again from the current light site and its commercial clarity, then brings the message up to date for a discovery environment shaped by AI.

Shared positioning

Lucky Domains helps businesses build a digital presence that people, search engines, and AI systems can find, understand, and trust.

Lucky Domains designs and builds websites, improves search and AI visibility, strengthens entity and brand signals, and secures the domains that anchor a business’s digital identity.

The Digital Presence Stack

  1. Domain Own the name and anchor the identity.
  2. Website Build the destination the business controls.
  3. Search Become technically sound, relevant, and discoverable.
  4. Entity Establish who you are and how everything relates.
  5. AI Become easier for AI systems to understand, verify, cite, and surface.

Five core services

  • Website Design & Development
  • SEO & Search Visibility
  • AI Visibility & Brand Authority
  • Entity SEO & Digital Identity
  • Domain Acquisition & Brokerage

AI is explained as a change in how the work is done (AI-powered execution with human accountability) and in where brands need to be recognized. Ken's agent experiments appear only as a small supporting section.

Concept A

Original, Evolved

The current Lucky Domains site, redesigned for 2027. Same white, green, and navy, same Plus Jakarta Sans headings, same direct founder-led service language, with a stronger hierarchy and diagrams that explain how the pieces fit.

  • White and warm off-white
  • Original full-color logo
  • Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter
  • Rounded cards and panels
  • Closest to today's site
Motion and interaction
A presence card in the hero lights the five layers in order and pauses on hover, focus, or its Pause button. The Digital Presence Stack settles slab by slab as each step scrolls into the reading line. A Where are you stuck? diagnostic reveals a recommended starting point.
Best suited for
Moving to production with the least risk. Existing customers will recognize it, and it keeps the consultation path obvious.
Tradeoff
The safest direction is also the least distinctive. It modernizes the site without giving Lucky Domains a new visual signature.

Concept B

Editorial Authority

A respected strategy, web, and visibility studio that explains its thinking. Warm paper, ink navy, a serif and sans pairing, numbered chapters, annotated case studies, and insights given real space.

  • Warm paper and ink
  • Newsreader and Public Sans
  • Chapters and margin notes
  • Annotated evidence
  • Built for articles
Motion and interaction
A hairline reading-progress bar and a section-aware contents rail on every inner page. A timeline of five eras of discovery fills as you read. A before-and-after site-structure view, and case-study annotations that highlight on hover, focus, or tap.
Best suited for
Building authority through writing: case studies, guides, and media credibility. Strongest for professional-services trust.
Tradeoff
It only works with good writing. Thin or infrequent articles would make it feel emptier than the others.

Concept C

Intelligent Presence

The most interactive direction. A living presence map shows how a domain, a website, content, search, structured data, profiles, reviews, founders, and citations come together into one identity AI systems can recognize.

  • Cool light ground
  • Manrope throughout
  • Interactive presence map
  • Modular tiles
  • Layer color coding
Motion and interaction
The map starts fragmented and settles into a recognized identity, then slowly tours one signal at a time, pausing on interaction. A problem selector lights the signals involved and names the service to start with. On the website page, an internal-link graph shows what each page links to.
Best suited for
Explaining entity, AI visibility, and identity work to visitors who learn by exploring. The clearest picture of how the layers connect.
Tradeoff
The most to build and maintain. The map must stay a teaching aid and not become a dashboard as content grows.
