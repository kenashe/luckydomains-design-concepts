01 · Website Design & Development

A website built to be found, understood, and trusted.

Lucky Domains designs and builds websites on your own domain, with the structure, performance, and technical foundation that search engines and AI systems rely on. Launch is the start of the site's job, not the end of ours.

Consult on the site you have

Ken inspects the existing site and writes down what is blocking search and conversion: structure, speed, indexing, page targeting, content gaps. You get a plain-English recommendation to fix or to rebuild, and what either would take.

Build or rebuild

Site structure, page templates, the core pages, and the technical foundation, launched on a real host and your own domain. Pages are built around what people actually search for, so the search work has something to stand on from day one.

  • Information architecture

    One page per job, organized the way customers search.

  • Performance

    Fast templates, compressed images, and nothing loaded that the page does not need.

  • Accessibility

    Semantic markup, keyboard support, readable contrast, and motion that respects user settings.

  • Technical SEO

    Clean URLs, self-referencing canonicals, sitemaps, and internal links that match them.

  • Structured data

    Organization, Person, service, and breadcrumb data that describes real facts.

  • Analytics foundations

    Measurement set up deliberately, with only what you need to make decisions.

  • Conversion and trust

    Clear paths to contact, honest proof, and nothing that feels like a trap.

  • Maintainability

    A handoff you can edit without us, documented in plain English.

The company site moved from Wix to a hand-built static site on GitHub Pages, with the duplicate-URL problems fixed and every decision written down in a public repository.

Read the case study

  • Moved the site from Wix to GitHub Pages on the apex domain, with HTTPS
  • Rewrote 93 internal links to one canonical form
  • Gave every page a self-referencing canonical tag that matches the sitemap
  • Kept old .html addresses as noindex stubs that point to the new URLs

    A daily agent researches listing sites and creates verified profiles. 63 sites researched, 8 profiles confirmed live. No SEO impact has been established, and most of these profiles carry nofollow links.

The Lucky Domains story

Domains were the beginning. Not the boundary.

Lucky Domains began with the most fundamental piece of digital identity: the domain. From there, the work expanded naturally into the website built on that name, the search visibility that brings people to it, and the entity and authority signals that help search engines and AI systems understand the brand.

The name still matters. It is now the first layer of something larger.

How AI changes the work

AI-powered execution with human accountability.

Lucky Domains uses AI and internal automation for the slow, repetitive parts of the job, so more of the time goes into judgment. Ken reviews and signs off on every recommendation.

  • Research

    Domain candidates, competitors, and the questions customers actually ask.

  • Technical audits

    Crawls, indexing checks, and canonical and duplicate-URL reviews.

  • Website QA

    Links, metadata, accessibility, and performance checks on every change.

  • Structured-data review

    Validation of Person, Organization, and service data against the facts.

  • Content architecture

    Page maps built from real search demand.

  • Profile and citation research

    Finding the profiles and sources that describe a brand, and where they disagree.

  • Implementation

    Repetitive markup, redirects, and page updates, checked by tests.

  • Monitoring and reporting

    Tracking changes over time and preparing plain-English reports.

Lucky Domains runs an internal agent that researches listing sites, creates profiles, and confirms its own verification emails. It is an internal system, not a client product.
