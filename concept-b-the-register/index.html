Lucky Domains is a founder-led digital venture studio building AI applications, digital identity systems, and internet-native products, while continuing its original domain acquisition and brokerage practice.

Feature · Venture No. 01 · Live

A business only AI agents can use.

An always-on service that sells two small jobs to other AI agents for 0.0001 ETH each. Agents find it through the ENS name zerohuman.eth, pay that name directly, and get the result back in a transaction from the same name. No account, no API key, no marketplace, and no human handling orders.

The name is the business. The ENS name is the storefront, the payment address, and the sender of every result. A text record on the name publishes the menu, and the worker refuses to sign anything unless the name, the menu, and its own wallet all agree. The name itself is owned by a separate cold wallet.

Ken Ashe wrote the specification and made the decisions involving money and identity. An AI agent wrote, tested, and shipped the code over about five days.

As of November 20, 2026, no outside agent has bought from it. Both paid orders so far came from the founder's own test buyer.

Standing notice · Active Service

The original practice continues.

The domain you want is rarely listed for sale. Ken values it, approaches the owner without revealing the buyer, negotiates against a range you agreed, and moves it through third-party escrow. He also represents sellers who want a confidential, managed exit from a name they no longer need.

  1. i.

    Set the range

    A fair-market range from comparable sales, traffic and keyword data, and strategic value, before anyone is contacted.

  2. ii.

    Reach the owner

    The conversation opens without revealing who the buyer is.

  3. iii.

    Negotiate

    Against the agreed range, not the seller's opening ask.

  4. iv.

    Close through escrow

    Funds release only when the domain is verifiably in the buyer's control.

The brokerage fee is success-based and agreed in writing before outreach. If there is no purchase, there is no success fee.
