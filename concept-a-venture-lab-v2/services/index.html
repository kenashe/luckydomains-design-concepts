01 · The original practice

ACTIVECommercial service

Domain Acquisition & Brokerage

Get the name that is already taken. Or sell one quietly.

The domain you want is rarely listed for sale. Ken values it, approaches the owner without revealing the buyer, negotiates against a range you agreed, and moves it through third-party escrow. He also represents sellers who want a confidential, managed exit from a name they no longer need.

  1. 01

    Set the range

    A fair-market range from comparable sales, traffic and keyword data, and strategic value, before anyone is contacted.

  2. 02

    Reach the owner

    The conversation opens without revealing who the buyer is.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Against the agreed range, not the seller's opening ask.

  4. 04

    Close through escrow

    Funds release only when the domain is verifiably in the buyer's control.

In scope

  • Target identification and fair-market valuation
  • Confidential, anonymous owner outreach
  • Price negotiation on your behalf
  • Escrow-backed, secure transfer
  • Expiring, auction, and backorder hunting
  • Portfolio strategy and domain consulting
  • Brokerage for sellers seeking a quiet sale

What this is not

  • Not a public auction or a bidding war in your name
  • Not a guaranteed close. Some owners will not sell
  • Not a quoted success rate. None is published
  • Not a retainer

Success-based fee. The brokerage fee is success-based and agreed in writing before outreach. If there is no purchase, there is no success fee.

02

Selective

Digital Identity & Presence

Make it unambiguous who, or what, is behind a name.

Search engines and AI systems organize the web around entities, not just keywords. This work gives a person, company, or product one canonical home and consistent, machine-readable facts: structured data with stable identifiers, profiles that agree with each other, and clear relationships between founder, company, and product. Lucky Domains also operates an onchain identity first-hand: zerohuman.eth is the storefront, payment address, and sender for a live service.

Google creates Knowledge Panels automatically. No one can guarantee one. This work improves the evidence Google and other systems have to work with.

Ask about fit

In scope

  • Canonical entity-home audit
  • Person and Organization structured data with stable identifiers
  • Profile mapping and consistency across the web
  • Founder, company, author, and product relationships
  • Naming across DNS and ENS (proposed scope)

By Fit

Selected Website & Entity Engagements

Taken on when a launch, an identity, or a venture needs it.

A site build or search engagement is in scope when it supports something larger: a name just acquired, a venture going live, an identity that needs a proper home. The work covers site structure, core pages, and the technical foundation, launched on a real host and your own domain. LuckyDomains.io and KenAshe.ai were shipped this way.

Lucky Domains is not a full-service marketing agency. No ads, no social, no open-ended retainers, and no visual-only redesigns.

Ask about fit

In scope

  • Consult on an existing site: what blocks search and conversion
  • Build or rebuild on a real host and domain
  • Technical foundation: canonicals, structured data, performance
  • A handoff you can edit without us
