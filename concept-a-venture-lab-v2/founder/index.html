Background

Ken started his career in accounting and financial reporting before moving into project, product, and program leadership. Over roughly two decades, that career ran through S&P Global, EY, Noom, and Prudential Financial, where he served as Director of Project and Product Management.

He built Lucky Domains alongside that work and still runs it himself. He later left corporate work to focus on entrepreneurship and building practical AI applications: agents, automations, and internet-native services, with the evidence published, failures included.

The credentials are operating evidence, not the headline. A CPA's instinct for controls and a project manager's delivery discipline show up in how the ventures are built: written specifications, spending limits, independent reviews, and limits stated plainly.

A CPA would call that a control. A project manager would call it a repeatable process. I call it the only sensible way to run this practice.
