Shared by all three

  • The production stylesheet, loaded unchanged, under each concept's own layer.
  • One message: Lucky Domains helps businesses build a digital presence that people, search engines, and AI systems can find, understand, and trust.
  • Domain, Website, Search, Entity, AI, presented as one system.
  • Five services, each with its own page, plus How we buy domains.
  • No guarantees of rankings, AI citations, or Knowledge Panels, with Google's and Bing's own documentation cited.
  • ZeroHuman and other experiments only in a small From the lab section and a labeled research note.
  • Noindex, forms that send nothing, keyboard access, and complete content without JavaScript or motion.
