SEO & Search Visibility

The fundamentals still decide who gets found.

And they now feed AI-powered search too.

Technical SEO

Crawlability, speed, Core Web Vitals, indexing, canonicals, structured data, and mobile health.

On-page optimization

Titles, headings, and copy matched to real search intent, so the right page wins the click.

Internal linking

Links that tell search engines which pages matter and how they relate.

Content strategy

A plan of pages and articles that answer the questions buyers actually ask.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, consistent local listings, and reviews, for businesses that serve a place.

Authority building

Earned, editorial references and digital PR. No rented links.

Search intent

Matching each page to what the searcher is actually trying to do.

Search Console and monitoring

Indexing, coverage, and queries watched over time, and reported in plain English.

Why SEO is expanding

AI search is built on search.

What the search engines themselves say.

Source: Google Search Central

Google says the same SEO fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode: crawlable pages, useful first-party content, internal links, good page experience, and structured data that matches the visible text. It also says there is no special schema and no new AI text file required.

Read the source
Source: Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing Webmaster Tools now includes an AI Performance report that shows which pages are cited in AI-generated answers in Copilot and Bing, and the grounding queries behind those citations. AI visibility is becoming measurable, at least in part.

How we work

Five steps, repeated.

Audit

Crawl the site, read Search Console, and find what blocks indexing and ranking.

Fix the foundation

Speed, indexing, canonicals, structured data, and mobile health.

Target the pages

Match each page to a real query and the intent behind it.

Build content

Pages and articles that answer the questions buyers ask.

Monitor

Track what changed, report it honestly, and adjust.

Honest reporting. Reporting is plain English and tied to the work done: what changed, what moved, and what did not. There are no ranking guarantees, and no one who offers them should be trusted.
