What entity SEO is

Your website can rank and still be misunderstood.

Traditional SEO helps pages rank. Entity SEO helps search engines understand the person or organization behind those pages: the canonical website, official profiles, founders, companies, authors, credentials, products, and credible references that all belong to the same real-world entity.

When those signals conflict or stay disconnected, systems struggle to tell one person or company from another. When they are consistent and machine-readable, they have a clearer picture to work with.

The evidence we work on

  • A clear canonical website
  • Accurate structured data
  • Consistent external profiles
  • Unambiguous person and company relationships
  • Credible third-party references
  • Consistent public facts
