Layer 01 · Domain

Get the name that's already taken

The right domain builds instant trust and anchors everything else: your site, your email, your profiles, and every reference to you. Lucky Domains helps you secure it, even when it is taken, and represents sellers who want a discreet, well-negotiated exit.

Set the range

A fair-market range from comparable sales, traffic and keyword data, and strategic value, before anyone is contacted.

Reach the owner

The conversation opens without revealing who the buyer is, so the price stays grounded in value.

Negotiate

Against the range you agreed, not the seller's opening ask. You approve or decline the price at every step.

Close through escrow

Funds release only when the domain is verifiably in your control.

Layer 04 · Entity

Help search engines and AI systems understand exactly who you are.

Traditional SEO helps pages rank. Entity SEO helps search engines understand the person or organization behind those pages: the canonical website, official profiles, founders, companies, authors, credentials, products, and credible references that all belong to the same real-world entity.

AI visibility and brand authority

What we will not promise

  • We do not guarantee rankings, AI citations, mentions in any particular AI system, or a Knowledge Panel. No one honestly can. We improve the evidence those systems work from, and we show you what changed.
  • There is no secret AI markup. Google says there is no special schema and no new machine-readable file needed for its AI features. Structured data helps systems read facts you can already show.
