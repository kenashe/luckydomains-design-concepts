Background

Builder, operator, accountant.

Ken started his career in accounting and financial reporting before moving into project, product, and program leadership. Over roughly two decades, that career ran through S&P Global, EY, Noom, and Prudential Financial, where he served as Director of Project and Product Management.

He built Lucky Domains alongside that work and still runs it himself. The person on your first call is the person who values the domain, scopes the site, runs the audit, and writes the report.

He also builds and documents AI systems in public at KenAshe.ai. That work keeps his judgment current, and some of it, like the internal agent that maintains profiles, runs inside Lucky Domains today.

A CPA would call that a control. A project manager would call it a repeatable process.
